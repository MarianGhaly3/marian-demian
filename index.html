From biology to browsers

I started out studying biology at Faculty of Sciences, then found my way into design and code — first as a graphic designer, later full-stack. Eight years on, I still work both sides of the brief: the interface someone clicks through, and the visual identity that makes it recognisable.

Day to day that means custom WooCommerce plugins for prescription eyewear, Shopify pricing calculators fed by live gold rates, Laravel back ends, and the logos, brochures and business cards that sit alongside them. I like projects where the code and the design have to agree with each other.
